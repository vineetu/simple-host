This page runs in your browser and never asks for a password or key: secrets appear as blanks for you to fill in, or on the AWS path are typed in AWS CloudShell. Nothing you type is sent anywhere, except where this server offers them, for the optional check just before your files and for the assistant when you ask it something. The check sends only the product and the names and values of the numbers, durations, switches, choices and limits you changed; the assistant sends those, the answers you picked from lists (such as your sign-in provider), where you are in the form, your message and its last few turns, and error output only when you paste it and click Send for help, after keys, tokens, passwords and email addresses are removed and you have seen what will go. Nothing from the form's hostnames, emails or secrets, and nothing about you. They go to our model provider to write the answer, and nothing is kept (privacy). Every setting is explained in the advanced docs: small box · Enterprise.
