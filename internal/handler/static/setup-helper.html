This page runs in your browser and never asks for a password or key: secrets appear as blanks for you to fill in. Nothing you type is sent anywhere, except for the optional check just before your files, where this server offers it: it sends only the product and the names and values of the numbers, durations, switches and limits you changed (never hostnames, emails or anything about you) to our model provider, and nothing is kept (privacy).
