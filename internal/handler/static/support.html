Support

Simple Host and Simple Hack · last updated 2026-10-01

Email support@simple-host.app.

It helps to include:

  • the email you sign in with;
  • the site or event address, for example my-site.you.simple-host.site or spring.simple-hack.app;
  • what you asked your AI app to do;
  • what happened instead.

Simple Hack

I need to manage an event or join my team.
Sign in at simple-hack.app/events. To join an event, open its organiser's join link while signed in. For event rules, judging decisions or a team invitation, contact that event's organiser.

My AI app is connected to the wrong role.
Reconnect https://simple-hack.app/mcp in the app and choose Manage my events for organising, or the relevant team for publishing its project. Event management and team publishing use separate connections.

I need to revoke a Simple Hack connection or delete my account.
Open simple-hack.app/account. Sign out everywhere revokes keys and connected apps. Account deletion is available on the same page. For event-record exports or removal help, email support with the event address.

Simple Host common fixes

My AI app says Simple Host isn't connected, or asks me to sign in again.
Open the app's connector or plugin settings and reconnect Simple Host. You will sign in once more on simple-host.app.

I want to disconnect an app.
Sign in at simple-host.app/dashboard. Under Connected apps on your sites page, choose Disconnect next to the app.

I need to remove a site.
Delete it from the dashboard, or ask your AI app to delete it. A deleted site is restorable for 7 days from Recently deleted on your page; then every version and its saved data are gone.

Saved data or an entry disappeared.
Open History or Recently deleted under the site's data on your page, or ask your AI app to restore it. Every change is kept for 30 days: the owner can put any of it back.

I want a copy of my data, or to delete my account.
Sign in at simple-host.app/dashboard. In Your data on your page, choose Download my data for one .zip file of everything, or Delete my account to delete the account and all its data at once. Deleting cannot be undone.

A site hosted here is illegal, abusive or a scam.
Use the report form, or email support@simple-host.app with the site's full address and what is wrong with it. See how reports are handled.

An intimate image of me is on a site here.
Email support@simple-host.app with the subject “Intimate image removal” and the page's address. See the Terms for what to include; we act within 48 hours.

A site copies my work.
Send a copyright notice as described in the Terms of Service.

A site collected my details and I want them removed.
Ask the site's owner first. If you cannot reach them, email support@simple-host.app with the site's address.
