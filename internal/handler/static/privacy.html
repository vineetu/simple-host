Who we are

Simple Host is run by Vineet Sriram, an individual (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what personal data the service at simple-host.app handles, why, and what you can do about it. It applies to people who publish sites, to people who visit them, and to people who use Simple Host from an AI app.

The rules for using the service are in the Terms of Service.

What we collect when you have an account

  • Email address. Used to sign you in. We get it when you enter a code we emailed you, or when Google confirms a verified address. It is your account name.
  • Google account ID. If you use Google, we keep Google's ID for you and the verified email it last sent, so you land in the same account next time. We do not keep your name, picture or Google password, or any access to your Google account.
  • Profile details you choose. Your handle and display name, if you set them.
  • API key and connection tokens. Used to prove requests come from you. We store tokens for AI apps only as one-way hashes.
  • Your sites. The files you upload, each version, the site name, and any domain or free <name>.simple-host.app address you connect.
  • Data your sites save. Lists and state that your pages or your AI app save.
  • Request records. IP addresses and times of requests, used for rate limits, security and investigating abuse. See how long we keep things.

If you visit a site hosted here

Sites on Simple Host are made by their owners, not by us. The owner decides what the site shows and what it asks you for.

What the site asks you for

  • It belongs to the site's owner. Anything you type into a site (an RSVP, a comment, a survey answer, an order) is collected by that site's owner. We store it for them. Ask the owner how they use it, or ask them to delete it.
  • Most saved data is public. Unless a list is private, anyone can read what you submit. Do not submit anything you would not want public.
  • Private lists. On a site with its own address, the owner can make a list private. Only the owner and the Simple Host operator can read it. The operator reads private lists only when needed for moderation, security, support or the law.
  • Your email goes with private submissions. To add to a private list you sign in, and your verified email is saved with what you submit. The site owner sees it.

Signing in on a site

Some sites ask you to sign in with Google or an emailed code before you save. That creates a Simple Host account under your email. The site gets a sign-in cookie for its own address, which lasts up to 30 days. The page never gets your API key, and nothing records your name on public lists.

One account is used for every site you sign in to. We can see that link. We do not show it to the sites.

Visit counts

When you open a page hosted here, we count the visit so the site's owner can see how many people read it.

  • Nothing is added to the page. There is no analytics script, no tracking cookie and no browser fingerprint. The count comes from our web server's own log.
  • The server log records the time, the address asked for, the result, your IP address and your browser's User-Agent. Log files are deleted after 30 days.
  • The analytics database does not keep your IP address. It keeps a scrambled version (a hash made with a secret key that stays on our server), the country your IP is in, the site, the hour, and whether the visit looks like a person, a bot or our own monitoring. The page path and User-Agent are not kept there.
  • The hash is a lasting identifier. The same connection produces the same hash on different days. That is how unique visitors are counted, and it means visits can be linked over time.
  • Countries are looked up on our server, using a local copy of a public database (IP Geolocation by DB-IP, CC BY 4.0). Your IP is not sent anywhere for this.
  • Who sees counts. Site owners see visit counts and countries for their own sites. We see them for all sites. Nobody is shown your IP address or the hash.
  • Analytics records are deleted after 400 days, or when the site is deleted. There is no opt-out, because blocking cookies or sending do-not-track does not change a server log.

When a page saves data (a comment, an RSVP), that is an API call. API calls record the raw IP address for 30 days, so abuse can be investigated. Only we can see those records.

Using Simple Host from an AI app

The Simple Host connector (also called a plugin; address https://simple-host.app/mcp) lets an AI app such as ChatGPT, Claude or Grok work on your sites for you.

  • Signing in. You sign in on simple-host.app and choose Allow. The app gets an access token that lasts one hour and a refresh token that it uses to get new ones. A refresh token is replaced each time it is used and expires after 90 days without use. Together they can publish, change and delete your sites and their data, and nothing else. We store only one-way hashes of them.
  • What we receive. Only what the app sends when it uses a Simple Host tool: site names, files, data to save, domain names and similar. We do not receive your chat history or the rest of your conversation.
  • What we send back. Depending on the tool: your account email, handle and display name; your sites, files, versions and addresses; a site's domain and its status; saved data (including private lists you own); and visit counts. We do not send internal IDs, IP addresses or visitor hashes. The app then handles that data under its own privacy policy.
  • What we keep. Connector requests are logged like other API calls, including the IP for 30 days. We keep a record of each connection (which app, when you connected, when it was last used) until you disconnect it. We do not record what you asked the app.
  • Disconnecting. Sign in at simple-host.app/dashboard and choose Disconnect under Connected apps. Rotating your API key also disconnects every app.

A coding agent that uses your API key works the same way: its requests are made as you.

Building with the AI in Simple Host

  • Create with AI. If you use the builder on simple-host.app, your prompt and any files you attach are sent to xAI's Grok to write the site. xAI handles them under its own terms and privacy policy. We do not save your prompt in our database.
  • Voice input. If you speak a prompt, the recording is turned into text on our own server and deleted straight after. It is not sent to anyone else.

How we use it

  • To sign you in, host and serve sites, and run the data each site saves.
  • To show site owners, and us, how many people and bots visit.
  • To enforce limits, keep the service secure, and investigate and act on abuse and reports.
  • To answer your emails and handle requests.
  • To follow the law and respond to lawful requests.

We do not sell personal data, share it for advertising, or use it to build advertising profiles. We do not send marketing email.

If privacy laws such as the GDPR apply to you, our legal bases are: running the service you asked for (contract), keeping it secure and free of abuse (legitimate interests), and meeting legal duties.

Who we share it with

  • Resend, our email provider, which sends sign-in codes. It gets your email address and the code.
  • Google, when you choose “Sign in with Google”. Google learns that you signed in to Simple Host.
  • xAI, only when you use Create with AI, as described above.
  • Our hosting and backup providers. The service runs on rented cloud servers. Backups are encrypted before they are stored with a cloud storage provider, so it cannot read them.
  • Site owners get what visitors submit to their sites, as described above.
  • Authorities. We may share information when we believe the law requires it, or to protect people or the service. We report child sexual abuse material as the law requires.
  • A new operator. If the service is transferred, for example to a company, your data goes with it under this policy.

No IP address is sent to any geolocation service. There are no other third parties.

Cookies

We use only cookies that are needed to sign you in, on simple-host.app and on sites where you sign in. There are no analytics or advertising cookies. The dashboard may also keep your sign-in in your browser's storage.

How long we keep things

  • Account: until you ask us to delete it.
  • Sites, versions and saved data: until you delete the site. Earlier versions are kept so you can roll back. Items in a private list can also be edited or deleted one by one by the owner.
  • Server logs: 30 days.
  • Raw IP addresses from API calls: 30 days.
  • Analytics records: 400 days, or until the site is deleted.
  • AI app connections: until you disconnect. Unused refresh tokens expire after 90 days.
  • Sign-in cookies on sites: up to 30 days.
  • Backups: deleted data can stay in encrypted backups for about 14 days.
  • Removed for abuse: we may keep content and account records longer when we must for legal reasons or to deal with abuse.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete a site yourself at any time, from the dashboard or your AI app. This removes its files, saved data and analytics.
  • Delete your account, or ask what we hold about you: email vineetu@gmail.com from the address you sign in with. We may need to confirm it is you.
  • Correct your details: email us and we will fix them.
  • Data a site collected from you: ask that site's owner first. If you cannot reach them, or the site is abusive, email us with the site's address.

Depending on where you live, you may have more rights, such as to get a copy of your data, object to how it is used, or complain to your data protection authority. Email us to use them. We answer within the time the law requires.

Security

We use HTTPS everywhere, store keys and tokens as hashes where we can, and limit access to the operator. No system is perfectly secure. Do not use Simple Host for sensitive data such as payment cards, health information, government ID numbers or passwords.

Where data is processed

Our servers are in the United States. Your data may be processed in the United States and in other countries where our providers work, which may have different privacy laws from yours.

Children

Simple Host is not directed at children under 13, or under the higher age your country sets. We do not knowingly collect their personal data. If you believe a child has given us personal data, email us and we will delete it.
