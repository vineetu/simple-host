What happens next

Your AI helps with the event and gives you the links to share. You can also manage your event, team or judging from Simple Hack on your phone.

Is it free?
Yes. Running an event, joining, judging and hosting team sites are free.
Who sees the scores?
After results are published, ranks and overall scores are public by default. Organisers can hide either. Teams privately see their own detailed scores and feedback; organisers see all results.
Do I need a laptop?
No. You can run, join or judge from a phone. Coding agents need a computer.
Where do team sites live?
At team.event.simple-hack.app, using your team and event names.
Can I run my own?
Yes. Self-host a hackathon with Simple Host.
Other ways to install

Download the five skill folders for your coding agent, or upload the skills-only plugin ZIP in a ChatGPT workspace that supports custom plugin uploads. Connect https://simple-hack.app/mcp as above to use them.
