How it works

1

Create your event

A page at <your-event>.simple-hack.app with the dates, rules and prizes.

2

Share the join link

People sign in with their email, agree to your code of conduct and form teams.

3

Add judges

Send them their own link. They join as judges, separate from the teams.

Free

There is nothing to pay. Create an event, share the link, run the weekend.
