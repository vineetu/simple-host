Build websites by talking to the AI you already use.

Add Simple Host to your AI once. Then ask for a site and it goes online.

Pick your AI

  1. In ChatGPT, open Settings and turn on Developer mode. It's under Security and login.
  2. In ChatGPT's app settings, add a new app called Simple Host. Paste this address, choose OAuth, and leave the advanced fields empty.
    https://simple-host.app/mcp
  3. A Simple Host window opens. Sign in with Google or a code we email you, then choose Allow.

Once it's approved, Simple Host will also be in ChatGPT's app directory.

Other ways to install

Try one of these

  • Build a small shop for my spice pantry, with a private order form only I can read

  • Make a site for my home cleaning business, with a booking form that saves privately

  • Create a resource hub for local families with a sign-up form, and show me who signed up

It's live

Your site is online at its own address, and it shows up under Your sites.

Who can see my site?

Anyone with the link can see its pages. Private lists, like orders or sign-ups, can only be read by you, on the site's own address.

Is it free?

Yes.

Can I use my own domain?

Yes. Or pick a free name.simple-host.app address.
