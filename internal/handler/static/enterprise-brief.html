Simple Host for companies · a brief

Stateful static websites, hosted inside your network.

Your people already make prototypes, dashboards and trackers with AI. Simple Host gives each of them an address inside your network to host them, with shared saved data and no server-side code, and gives the company control over all of it.

The problem

What people make in ChatGPT, Claude and other AI tools ends up as links with random IDs on hosts outside the company. They are hard to find again, hard to tell apart, and nobody owns the list.

What the addresses look like

Each person's work sits under their own name, on your domain, so it reads like a portfolio rather than a list of IDs.

alex-kim.your-internal-domain.com/sales-dashboard
alex-kim.your-internal-domain.com/onboarding-checklist
maria-lopez.your-internal-domain.com/offsite-signup

What it is

An address per person

Everyone gets their own hostname, and each thing they make gets a readable address under it. Colleagues find it by the author's name.

Pages that keep data

Saved data is shared between the people using a page, survives restarts, and works from any browser or phone. Several people can edit it.

No server-side code

Pages run in the reader's browser. The server stores pages and data and runs nothing that people publish.

Inside your network

You run it on your own servers, behind your VPN, with your own sign-in: Okta or anything that speaks OIDC.

Access

Pages are unlisted by default: only people with the link can open them. Name specific people when a page must be closed to everyone else.

Governance

Admins see every site, who made it, and who can open it. Every change and every visit is recorded.

Light to run

One small service, a Postgres database and file storage. Hosting something that already works takes the AI a single short step, so it costs few tokens.

Yours to extend

Your engineers have the code. Add what you need, for example scanning pages for confidential data.
