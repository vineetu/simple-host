Organiser

Create the event, open sign-up, and share the join and judge links.

On the website

  1. Sign in at simple-hack.app with Google or an email code.
  2. On Home, click Create event, fill in the details, then click Create event again to save it as a draft.
  3. From the sidebar, open Judging to write your rubric, and Teams, then its Tracks tab, to add tracks if you want them.
  4. On Overview, click your next step when you're ready. It walks you from opening sign-up through to publishing results.
  5. Under Share your event, click Copy next to your join link and judge link, and send them out.

In your AI app

Add Simple Hack to your AI once (see Pick your AI below), then ask in a new chat:

Create a hackathon called Night Build for next Saturday and Sunday, and give me the join link.

Your AI may ask for event details or confirmation before opening an event.

Participant

Join the event, find a team, and publish your team's site.

On the website

  1. Open the join link the organiser sent you, then sign in with Google or an email code.
  2. Fill in your name, answer any questions, check I agree to the code of conduct, then click Join event (or Send application, if the event needs approval).
  3. On Team, choose how you want to team up: Start a new team, Ask to join a team, or Join with a link or code.
  4. On Entry, fill in your project's details and click Save draft.
  5. On Website, click Upload to publish a zip of your site yourself. No AI app needed.
  6. Back on Entry, click Submit entry. Some events need a website first.

In your AI app

Add Simple Hack to your AI once (see Pick your AI below), then describe your idea:

Join the hackathon with code ab12cd34 and start a team called Night Owls. Build our team’s site: an app that matches volunteers to shifts.

Use your event’s real join code. Your AI builds the page and publishes it to your team's Website tab.

Judge

Accept the judge invite, then score your assigned teams.

On the website

  1. Open the judge link the organiser sent you, then sign in with Google or an email code.
  2. Read the code of conduct, type your name, check I agree to the code of conduct, then click Join as judge.
  3. Read the brief under Rubric & brief.
  4. Open My teams and click Score this team.
  5. Tap a score for each part of the rubric, add an optional comment, then click Save & next (Save & finish on your last team).

In your AI app

Add Simple Hack to your AI once (see Pick your AI below), then ask in a new chat:

I’m judging Night Build. Show me my next entry.

You’ll need the organiser’s judge link first, the same as on the website.

ChatGPTOn the web

Add custom MCP server

The fastest way: one address to paste.

  1. Open ChatGPT Plugins.
  2. Click Add, then choose Add custom MCP server.
  3. Name it Simple Hack, paste this address, and save.
    https://simple-hack.app/mcp
  4. A sign-in window opens. Sign in with Google or an email code, then choose Allow.

ChatGPT keeps the tool list from the day you added Simple Hack. When Simple Hack adds features, open ChatGPT Plugins, choose Simple Hack, then Refresh.

Or add it by its address, in developer mode:

  1. Open Settings, then Apps & Connectors, then Advanced settings, and turn on Developer mode.
  2. Back in Apps & Connectors, choose Create.
  3. Name it Simple Hack, and paste this address as the MCP server URL:
    https://simple-hack.app/mcp
  4. Choose OAuth for authentication, then Create.
  5. Sign in with Google or an email code, then choose Allow.

What happens next

Manage your event, team or judging from simple-hack.app on any device, or from your AI app once it's connected. Your AI gives you the links to share.

Is it free?
Yes. Running an event, joining, judging and hosting team sites are free.
Who sees the scores?
After results are published, ranks and overall scores are public by default. Organisers can hide either. Teams privately see their own detailed scores and feedback; organisers see all results.
Do I need a laptop?
No. You can run, join or judge from a phone. Coding agents need a computer.
Where do team sites live?
At team.event.simple-hack.app, using your team and event names.
Can I run my own?
Yes. Self-host a hackathon with Simple Host.
Other ways to install

Optional: Simple Hack's skill files for coding agents. They add instructions, not the connection — connect https://simple-hack.app/mcp as above first.

  1. Install the five Simple Hack skills for Claude Code, Codex and other coding agents, then restart your agent:
    npx -y skills add https://github.com/vineetu/simple-host/tree/main/hack-toolkit --global --agent codex claude-code --yes
    For another agent, replace codex claude-code with its name, such as openclaw or hermes-agent.

Or download the skill folders directly, or upload the skills-only plugin ZIP in a ChatGPT workspace that supports custom plugin uploads.
