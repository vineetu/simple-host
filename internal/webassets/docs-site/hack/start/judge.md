# Judge

1. Open the organiser's judge link, sign in, and accept the invitation.
2. Open **Rubric & brief** (**Rubric** on a phone) and read the criteria before scoring. Press **I've read it** when done.
3. Open **My teams**, choose an assigned team, and open its entry and website. In **Teams I know**, declare a conflict for anyone you know, or press **I don't know any of these teams**.
4. Enter scores and feedback on the scoring screen; scores save immediately. Choose **Save & next**, or **Save & finish** on the last team. Finish before judging closes.


- [Connect your AI app](/get-started)

Use `hack_get_checklist` with the event slug and `role: "judge"` for current tasks and the next action. Confirm changes before writing.

Scoring screens say **Scoring not open** until the organiser starts Judging. Scoring every team in your queue also completes the rubric checklist task.

**I've read it** records the version shown with the rubric. If the organiser changes it before you confirm, the acknowledgement is refused and the updated rubric loads for you to read.
