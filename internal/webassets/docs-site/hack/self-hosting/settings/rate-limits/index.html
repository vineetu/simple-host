Rate limits

Each rate limit is <burst>,<every>: that many requests at once, then one more every <every> (a duration: 500ms, 5s, 6m, 1h). RATE_LIMIT_SIGNIN_IP=20,5s allows 20 sign-in requests from one address at once, then one every 5 seconds. Limits are per client address unless the description says otherwise.

Security-sensitive limits (sign-in, visitor sign-in, the connector’s OAuth) can be made stricter freely but at most four times looser than the default; anything looser stops the server at startup. The others may be set to anything, with a startup warning past ten times looser.

Burst must be 1–100,000 and <every> 1ms–24h, within any stricter security ceiling. Security-sensitive limits also include email codes, site passcode tries and spam-checked posts. Their burst may be at most four times the default and <every> at least a quarter of it; startup errors name the ceiling. Other limits log WARNING beyond ten times looser. Unknown RATE_LIMIT_* names are ignored with a startup WARNING.

RATE_LIMIT_SITE_OPS also covers account deletion; RATE_LIMIT_EXPORT includes link downloads. RATE_LIMIT_EVENT_APPLY_USER permits five applications an hour by default. Visitor OAuth limits cover both starts and callbacks. Passcode limits trigger the site lockout periods.

RATE_LIMIT_SIGNIN_IP=80,1250ms
RATE_LIMIT_UPLOAD=120,2s
RATE_LIMIT_STATE=240,250ms
