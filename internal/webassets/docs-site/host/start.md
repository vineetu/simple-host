# Your first site

1. Connect your AI app.

   [Connect your AI app](/install.html)

2. Ask for a site. Pick a prompt and add your details:
   - “Make a page for my bakery with opening hours and a contact form.”
   - “Make an RSVP page for Saturday's dinner.”
   - “Publish my portfolio with these three projects.”
3. Hand out the address your AI app gives you, exactly as given.
4. Change it from chat: “On my bakery site, change Saturday's closing time to 6 pm.”
