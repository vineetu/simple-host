How Simple Host fits together

Publish a website from your files or your AI app. Choose who can open it and what it can save.

Your site

Publish and update

Give your pages an address and keep earlier versions.

  • Files and versions

    Send a folder as an archive, upload files, or ask your connected AI app to publish them. Each update goes live at the same address. You can switch back to a version you published earlier.

    Connect your AI app
  • Site addresses

    Every site gets its own address. Give it a short name, connect a domain you own, or use one domain for all your sites.

    Your public page can list the sites you choose or open one of them as your home page.

    See the address formats
  • Your dashboard

    Find a site, take it offline, or put it back online. If you delete a site by mistake, you can restore it from the bin.

    If you run your own copy, the admin page also shows reports, network use and new accounts.

    Open your dashboard Run your own copy

Choose who has access

Decide who can run your site and who can visit.

  • Accounts and teams

    Sign in with a code sent to your email. Keep your own sites in your account. To manage sites together, make a team and invite people by email.

    From your account you can request a download of your data. The download link comes by email.

    Read about teams
  • Apps and keys

    Approve an AI app to let it act with your permissions. You can also create an API key. Disconnect the app or revoke the key in your account.

    Read about API keys
  • Visitors and passcodes

    Let anyone open the site, protect it with a passcode, or invite specific email addresses. On an invite-only site, someone outside the list can ask you for access.

    See how visitors sign in

Keep data and count visits

Save what visitors send and see which pages they read.

  • Tables, values and files

    A guestbook can keep each message in a table. A menu can save its opening hours as a named value. A form can accept a file.

    Only the owner has access by default. Set rules to let visitors read or write. For example, a guest can edit just their own message.

    Use saved data on a page
  • Owner alerts

    Get a message in your AI app or a push notification in your browser when selected data changes or someone asks for access.

  • Visit numbers

    See daily visits, popular pages, countries and where readers came from. Your own visits are left out. Bots are counted separately.

    Find the site stats route
