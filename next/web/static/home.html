Saved data that is yours

Every site can keep tables, values and files. You decide who may read and add. Ask your AI "show my bookings" and it reads them for you.

You choose who sees it

Open to anyone, locked with a passcode, or only for people you name by email. Visitors sign in with Google or an emailed code.

Your own domain

Every site works at once on its own address. Connect your own domain when you want one, and HTTPS comes up by itself.

Run your own copy

Install it on a small server with one Docker Compose command. See Get started.
