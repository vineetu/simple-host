Deleting your account removes everything at once: sites, data, keys, sign-ins and app connections. A deleted site stays 7 days in a bin, where you can restore it, then it is gone.

Reports and take-downs

To report a page, use the form on the Support page, or email support@simple-host.app with the address and what is wrong. We look at every report.

We can take down a site. It then shows "taken down" at every address. We can also suspend an account, which takes down all its sites. We may do either at any time, and without warning for serious harm. Both can be undone.

We never allow content that sexually exploits children. We remove it, close the account and report it to the authorities.

Intimate images shared without consent come down within 48 hours of a valid request. Email us with the subject "Intimate image removal", the exact address, your name, and a statement that the image shows you and was shared without consent.

For copyright, email us the address of the page, what was copied, and proof you hold the right. For security problems, email us with the subject "Security". Test only your own sites and give us time to fix it. Police and courts: use the subject "Legal request".
