Support

Write to support@simple-host.app. It helps to include:

  • the email you sign in with
  • the address of the site
  • what you expected, what happened, and any error message

Your AI app can help too: it can do anything you can do in Your sites.

Common questions

My AI app says Simple Host is not connected.

Reconnect Simple Host in the app's connector settings. You sign in once more.

I want to disconnect an app.

Open Your sites and remove it under Connected apps.

I deleted a site by mistake.

Deleted sites stay in a bin for 7 days. Restore one, with its saved data, from Your sites.

I want a copy of my data, or to delete my account.

Both are in Your sites, under Your data. A download comes as a link by email. Deleting an account removes everything at once.
