Support

Email support@simple-host.app. Please include:

  • the email you sign in with
  • the address of the site
  • what you expected, what happened, and any error message

You can also ask your AI app. It can do what you can do in the dashboard.

Common questions

My AI app says Simple Host is not connected.

Reconnect Simple Host in the app's connector settings. You sign in once more.

I want to disconnect an app.

Open your dashboard and remove it under connected apps.

I deleted a site by mistake.

Deleted sites stay in a bin for 7 days. Restore one from the dashboard, with its saved data.

I want a copy of my data, or to delete my account.

Both are in the dashboard under Your data. A download comes as a link by email. Deleting an account removes everything at once.
