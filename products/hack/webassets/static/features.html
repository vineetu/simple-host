Set up the event

  • Event page

    Set the dates and describe the challenge. Add the schedule, tracks and prizes to the page you share with participants.

    Create an event
  • Registration

    Open sign-up and share the join link. Ask participants questions when they sign up and choose whether to review applications.

    See how to open sign-up
  • Announcements

    Post a change of plan or a reminder for participants. Pin an update to keep it in view.

Build and submit

  • Teams

    Start a team or ask to join one with room. If a teammate sends you a join link, you can use it to find their team.

    See how to join a team
  • Team website

    Publish your project at your team’s own web address. Upload your site files or connect an AI app to build and publish the site with you.

    Connect your AI app
  • Project entry

    Describe the problem your team tackled and who the project helps. Save a draft, add the links your event asks for and submit before your deadline.

    See how to submit an entry

Judge and announce

  • Judging rubric

    Organisers set the scoring criteria. Judges open each entry, give points for each criterion and can leave a comment.

    See how to score an entry
  • People’s choice

    Organisers can open a vote on the entries and choose who can take part. Voters can change their pick until voting closes.

    Find a hackathon
  • Published results

    Review the scores and name the winners. Publish the results when you are ready for people to see them on the event page.
