Teams

A team owns sites, like a person does. Every member can publish, change and delete all of its sites, invite people and remove members, and delete the team once it owns no sites. People join by accepting an invitation. Members use their own sign-in; in your AI app, name the team as the owner of a site.

Sign in on your sites page first.

New team

The name becomes part of every team site's address, like your own handle. Lowercase letters, numbers and hyphens; no dots. It cannot be changed later.

Members

They join once they accept, signed in with that email; they find the invitation on this page. No email is sent, so tell them. Invitations lapse after 14 days.

Delete this team

Only a team that owns no sites can be deleted (sites in Recently deleted count). Its name is never given out again.
