- v0.7.0: Saved data, steps 3 and 4: two more kinds. **Personal** (`mine`): one private record per signed-in visitor per name (a habit tracker, saved progress, preferences) that follows them to any device; only that visitor reads or changes it (`SH.data(name, 'personal')`: `get`, `set`, `patch`, `inc`, `clear`, and their own 30-day `history`/`restore`; `GET`/`PUT`/`PATCH`/`DELETE /v1/sites/{s}/data/{name}`), at most 64 KB each; the site owner sees how many people have one and their size, never what they saved (the owner's key, connector, CSV, History, Recently deleted, Block by entry and the site download all refuse or leave it out: 403 `personal_data`), and can clear it for everyone (Restore brings back only what the clear took).
