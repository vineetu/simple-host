- **simple-hack.app M3: judging.** Organisers build a scoring rubric (ordered criteria with a name, description, weight and points scale; weights sum to 100), spread judges over teams openly or with a balanced, conflict-aware automatic assignment, and watch coverage on a judging dashboard. Judges and organisers can flag a conflict of interest, which drops that pairing from assignment and from totals. A judge's queue lists their eligible teams least-covered first; the scoring screen saves one or more criteria at a time (a retried save never double-counts) plus one comment per team. Each judge's score is Σ(weight/100 × points/max_points) × 100; a team's total is the mean across every judge who scored it, excluding conflicted judges. The organiser locks scoring (further writes refused until an organiser unlocks with a reason, which is logged) and publishes results: the ranking is computed and snapshotted, ties are flagged, and a tie can be resolved with per-team rank overrides; the public view defaults to winners only and can be switched to a full ranking at any time without recomputing. Each team privately sees its own total, rank and every judge's comment (never whose) after results are published. Raw scores and computed results both export as CSV. New tables `rubric_criteria`, `event_assignments`, `event_conflicts`, `event_scores`, `event_results` (`db/migrations/hack3-judging.sql`, also in `db/schema.sql`); new event columns `judge_assignment_mode`, `judges_per_team`, `judging_locked_at`, `judging_lock_reason`.
