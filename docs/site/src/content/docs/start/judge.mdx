<Steps>

1. Open the organiser's judge link, sign in, and accept the invitation.
2. Open **Rubric & brief** (**Rubric** on a phone) and read the criteria before scoring.
3. Open **My teams**, choose an assigned team, and open its entry and website.
4. Enter scores and feedback on the scoring screen; scores save immediately. Choose **Save & next**, or **Save & finish** on the last team. Finish before judging closes.

</Steps>
