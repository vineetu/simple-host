About

Generation Girl Online Hack is a free weekend hackathon for young women and girls in Indonesia. Bring an idea that helps your community and build it with AI and your team. No experience needed.

FAQ

Do I need to know how to code?

No. Bring an idea and use AI tools to help you build it. Mentors are on hand all weekend.

Does it cost anything?

No, Generation Girl Online Hack is free to join, including all mentoring sessions.

Can I join without a team?

Yes. Team formation happens at the start of the event — come with or without a team.

Rules

Build your project during the event. Credit the tools and sources you use. Be kind to everyone.
