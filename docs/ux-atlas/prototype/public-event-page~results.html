About

Young women and girls building with AI, one weekend, online. Bring an idea that helps your community and build it with your team.

28–29 Oct 2026 · Online · Asia/Jakarta (WIB)

Rules

Build during the event. Credit the tools and sources you use. Treat everyone with respect and follow the code of conduct.

Prizes

Recognition for the winning ideas, plus a People's choice award.

FAQ

Do I need to know how to code?

No. Bring your idea and use AI to help you build it.

Where does the event take place?

Online. All schedule times are in WIB.

Can I join without a team?

Yes. Meet other participants during team formation.
