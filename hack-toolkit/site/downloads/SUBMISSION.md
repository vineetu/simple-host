## Hack privacy data map for legal-page update

A Hack-specific policy should describe account email/name and sign-in, organiser event details and contact email, participant and judge membership, team names and sites, project entries and files, private judge assignments/scores/comments, OAuth client/grants and scopes, usage logs, and exports. Explain who can read each kind of data: organisers see their event's management information and exports; judges see assigned judging material; participants see their own team; public event and published team pages are public. State the actual collection, use, sharing, retention, deletion and support arrangements from the server's behavior. Future voting, announcement email and directory features should be added only when implemented. This map is engineering input, not published policy text or a legal attestation.
